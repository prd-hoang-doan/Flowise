Northwind Analytics Reports First Quarter Fiscal 2026 Results

SAN FRANCISCO — May 1, 2026 — Northwind Analytics, Inc. (NASDAQ: NWND), a leading provider of AI‑powered enterprise analytics, today announced financial results for its first fiscal quarter ended March 31, 2026.

First Quarter Fiscal 2026 Highlights

  • Total revenue of $342.7 million, an increase of 18% year‑over‑year.
  • Subscription revenue of $298.4 million, an increase of 22% year‑over‑year.
  • Annualized Recurring Revenue (ARR) of $1.34 billion, up 21% year‑over‑year.
  • GAAP operating margin of 11.4%; non‑GAAP operating margin of 23.6%, an expansion of 320 basis points year‑over‑year.
  • Non‑GAAP diluted earnings per share of $0.47, compared to $0.31 in the prior‑year quarter.
  • Customers contributing more than $100,000 in ARR grew to 1,287, an increase of 24% year‑over‑year.
